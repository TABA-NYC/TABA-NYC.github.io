About

Hi, I'm Tyler.

I design experiences for people. Mostly software, but sometimes visual, voice, and hardware too. I thrive in creative settings, the places that let me find flow; that's where I ideate and figure out how to move things forward. Currently consulting on product design and AI at Northwell Health.

How I Work

Creativity and problem solving are the core of how I operate. I start with understanding the problem, and lead with user research and discovery to uncover the "why". I'm highly collaborative and love working closely with users, subject matter experts, stakeholders, and teams to drive decisions and move products forward.

I'm a product design lead with a traditional visual design background, and consider myself a design generalist. I love getting my hands on the work and leading by example, but I have a passion for mentoring and helping designers grow their skills and thrive in their careers. It's the part of the job I'd keep if I could only keep one.

Where I've Operated

Over nine years across proptech, fintech, healthcare, and B2B SaaS, usually on the dense, operational products where design earns its keep. I've been the first design hire more than once, built design functions and their processes from the ground up, and led teams through platform pivots and org changes. I like challenging problems that build real value around products serving a need and a purpose.

Today that means AI: designing contract automation and workflow tooling for a major healthcare procurement organization, and shaping AI products people can actually trust and use.

Outside the Work

My creativity spans not only designing for software but expands into art and painting, interior design, and music. I play in a band that writes originals and performs covers.

I also mentor designers on ADPList and Designlab: portfolios, career transitions, and design leadership. If you're working on something operational, dense, or ambiguous, feel free to reach out: tpruyn@gmail.com.
