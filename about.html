How I Work

I start with the people doing the work: interviews, shadowing, and a hard look at the tools they reach for when the official software fails them. The patterns there (the spreadsheet kept on the side, the five tabs open to answer one question) are usually the real brief.

I lead with outcomes, not screens. I want to know what has to be true for the business and the person using the product, then design the smallest thing that makes it true. On a deadline that often means shipping the right 80 percent. A structurally pure solution that misses the season is worth less than a pragmatic one that ships, earns trust, and buys the next quarter to go deeper.

I do my best work alongside engineering and product. The projects I'm proudest of came from naming the real constraint early (a data migration that was too large, a hardware limit, a client's hard deadline) and designing within it instead of pretending it away.

What I'm Doing Now

I'm the design lead on contract automation for a $6B healthcare procurement organization. We're replacing a legacy enterprise interface with a modern contract lifecycle experience, and layering AI review on top: extraction running at 95 percent accuracy as the rollout standard, and legal review and negotiation time cut to under a third of baseline. The design problem I find most interesting here is making AI output something a busy reviewer can trust at a glance, and act on without second-guessing.

Where I've Been

At Darwin Homes I was the first design hire and helped grow the business to roughly three times its annual recurring revenue. I rebuilt the leasing CRM that ran the company's busiest workflow, which unlocked a 3,000-home institutional release and about a 30 percent monthly revenue increase, and I designed the renewals and resident-departure experience around it.

At ButterflyMX I worked on the hardware and software at the front door of thousands of buildings. I helped reimagine the smart intercom that shipped to about 99 percent of the fleet, and I re-architected the resident app from a simple door-opener into a daily habit. That information architecture still structures the live app, which holds a 4.8 App Store rating across more than 99,000 ratings.

The throughline across all of it: I like the unglamorous, high-stakes surfaces. The CRM, the intercom, the contract record. The places where good design is the difference between a tool people avoid and a tool people run their day on.
