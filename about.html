About

Hi, I'm Tyler.

I design experiences for people. Mostly software, but sometimes visual, voice, and hardware too. For the last ten years that focus has been product design and systems across B2B SaaS, in proptech, fintech, and healthcare. Right now I'm consulting on product design and AI at Northwell Health.

Where It Started

I was a born creative. I started in visual design, running my own business making graphics and designing shirts, and moved into product design when I realized user research could inform product decisions. The craft changed; the instinct didn't. It has always been about making something people actually want to use.

How I Work

I'm a strong collaborator. I like making decisions with the people who know best in their domains, so I go looking for the subject matter experts early and design with them, not around them. I start with the people doing the work: interviews, shadowing, and a hard look at the tools they reach for when the official software fails them. The spreadsheet kept on the side is usually the real brief.

And I love managing designers. Growing someone's craft and their soft skills at the same time, then watching them carry a project you would have once held onto yourself, is the best part of the job.

The Range

The work has stretched wide on purpose. I've led brand and logo redesigns with rollout plans across entire companies. I've done sound design for interaction patterns on keypads and hardware. I've designed voice interfaces, starting with The Economist's first Alexa skill. Mobile, web, voice, hardware: the medium changes, the job stays the same, solving the problem in front of the user and fitting the need.

What I'm Doing Now

I'm the first UX hire on contract automation for a $6B healthcare procurement organization. We're replacing a legacy enterprise interface with a modern contract lifecycle experience and layering AI on top: extraction running at 95 percent accuracy as the rollout standard, and legal review time cut to under a third of baseline. The problem I find most interesting is making AI output something a busy reviewer can trust at a glance.

Where I've Been

At Darwin Homes I went from Lead Product Designer to Head of Design, rebuilt the leasing CRM that ran the company's busiest workflow (unlocking a 3,000-home institutional release), and designed the AI assistant platform that replaced a third-party vendor at $150K a year. At ButterflyMX I was the first design hire, grew the team from zero to three, and worked on the hardware and software at the front door of thousands of buildings, including the resident app whose architecture still structures the live product at a 4.8 App Store rating across 99K+ ratings.

Outside the Work

The creativity doesn't stop at software. I paint and make artwork. I play in a band that writes originals and performs covers. I build furniture for my home and I'm the one who insists on doing the interior decorating. Different materials, same practice: make the thing fit the need.

I also mentor designers on ADPList and Designlab: portfolios, career transitions, and design leadership. If you're working on something operational, dense, and a little intimidating, that's exactly the kind of problem I like. Reach me at tpruyn@gmail.com.
